⚠️ How to read the results: this checks each address against its mail server without sending a real email. "Invalid" means the address is very likely dead (bad format, no mail server, or an explicit rejection). "Unknown" is common and doesn't mean the email is bad — many providers (Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo) refuse to confirm individual mailboxes this way, and some networks block this kind of check entirely. Treat "valid" as a good sign, "invalid" as safe to drop, and "unknown" as your own judgment call.
